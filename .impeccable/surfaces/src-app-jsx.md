Scope: the whole MalaMaster app (Practice, Orders, Top 10). Mode: Operate.
Audience/job: friends who eat mala; build a bowl by dragging ingredients like at the stall, learn weight and cost.
Constraints: phone first, one-handed; both pricing models; real averaged SG prices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The stall's laminated price board and steel ingredient trays are the interface; you physically drag pieces from trays into your basket. Refuses the grid-of-buttons tap game.
OWN-WORLD: Chili-oil red (#b3201c) header and accents, warm laminated cream ground (#fff4e0), brushed steel trays (#c8ccce gradient) holding white ingredient chips, near-black ink (#231a17), turmeric amber (#ffb000) for money. Tier price strip ruled in ink like a laminated board. One heavy grotesk (Archivo), tabular numerals.
STORY: Player sees what each tier costs, drags pieces into the basket, watches grams and S$ climb, learns what a budget looks like, then plays orders and posts to the top 10.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Red header with app name, mode tabs, monumental weight and S$ total; ruled tier price strip below; steel trays scroll underneath; the red woven basket docked at the bottom as the drop target with its own count.
FORM: Stall Price Board, impeccable's own #1 grounded candidate (pick card). seed:0141c69e8393a2bc
Signature interaction: drag a piece from a tray, it lifts and follows the finger, drops into the basket (springs back to the tray if released elsewhere). Tap still adds one, for accessibility.
